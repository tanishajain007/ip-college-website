About College

I.P. College of Education — a trusted B.Ed. institution in Haryana

Located at Jassia, District Rohtak, the college is affiliated to Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak and run by Arya Siksha Samiti, Jassia, Rohtak. Courses follow NCTE guidelines.

  • Human values, discipline, teamwork and personality development
